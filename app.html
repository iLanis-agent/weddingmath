The two numbers that run the day

Per-head = food + drink per guest at your venue tier (backyard ~$40, standard ~$95, black-tie ~$180+). Everything else is split by planner-standard weights, contingency included.

The line items that ambush couples

Service charges and tax on the catering quote (often +25% on top of the plate price). Vendor meals - your photographer and DJ eat too, at vendor-plate prices. Alterations, which live outside the dress budget. Overtime rates when the party runs one hour past the contract. The marriage license itself, the officiant, and the rehearsal dinner nobody budgeted. And the tips: hair, makeup, drivers, delivery crews - a stack of envelopes that adds up to a category of its own, which is why the split above carries misc and a real contingency instead of a round number that pretends to be exact.
